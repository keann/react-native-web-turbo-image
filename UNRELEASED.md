### ✨ Added

- **TurboImage**, the default export, which implements `react-native-turbo-image`'s component and
  its static methods on react-native-web.
- The types that `react-native-turbo-image` exports: `CachePolicy`, `Completion`, `Failure`,
  `Format`, `Indicator`, `IndicatorStyle`, `Progress`, `ResizeMode`, `Source`, `Start`, `Success`,
  and `TurboImageProps`.
- `pending:` source URIs, which hold an image in its loading state for stories and tests.
